Privacy

Frame, for iPad. Last updated 25 October 2026.

Frame collects nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and Frame sends nothing about you anywhere. It goes online only to ask the App Store about its one in-app purchase, and to fetch a video you open from iCloud Drive that isn't on your iPad yet.

Your videos

Frame opens the videos you choose from Files or iCloud Drive and plays them where they are. It does not copy or upload them. To reopen a video later it keeps a bookmark to the file, the point you reached and a small picture of that moment, all on your iPad. Removing a video from the list deletes these.

The frames you grab

Grabbed frames are held on your iPad until you clear the tray or close the app. They go only where you send them: the clipboard, another app you drag them into, Photos or Files when you save them, or wherever you share them.

Photos

If you save a frame to Photos, Frame asks for permission to add to your library. It can add pictures but cannot see the photos already there.

Purchases

If you buy Unlimited Videos, Apple handles the payment through the App Store. Frame never sees your payment details or your Apple Account; it only asks the App Store whether the purchase has been made.
