Frame

Hold on a video to lift the frame straight into your notes.

Grabbing a frame

Hold your finger on the video for about half a second, playing or paused. The frame that was showing when your finger landed goes to the clipboard and to the tray. You can also tap Grab, or press Command-Shift-C on a keyboard.

Dragging it into another app

Put Frame beside your notes app in Split View, a window or Slide Over. Hold on the video until the frame lifts, keep holding, move it over the other app and let go. If an app won't take the drop, the frame is already on the clipboard: tap where you want it and choose Paste.

A video won't open

Frame plays MP4, MOV and M4V files from Files and iCloud Drive. A video stored only in iCloud is downloaded first, which can take a moment on a slow connection. Videos protected by copy protection, such as purchases from a store, can't be opened.

Where frames go

Frames wait in the tray until you clear it or close the app. From the tray you can drag, copy, save to Photos or Files, or share any of them. Frame never copies your videos: it plays them where they are and remembers only where you got to.

Three videos free, or Unlimited Videos

Frame keeps three videos in its list for free, archive included. When you open a fourth it asks you to remove one, which only takes it off Frame's list and leaves the file where it is, or to buy Unlimited Videos, a single in-app purchase that lifts the limit for good. It is not a subscription. On a new iPad, or after reinstalling, tap Restore Purchase in Settings.
